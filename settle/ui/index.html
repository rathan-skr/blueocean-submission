Broker partitions and their consumers

Each merchant's events always go to the same partition, so they stay in order. Each partition is owned by exactly one consumer at a time; "lag" is how many messages are waiting.

Channels behind

Received = in order from the gateway. Applied = in the ledger. A receive gap means the reader is waiting for a resend.

Payouts by state

"Unknown" means the bank may have paid it (a 502 or a timeout). It is never sent again blindly: after 18 s the bank is searched, then it becomes paid, or is resent with a new key.
